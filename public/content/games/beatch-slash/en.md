Your boyfriend is gorgeous. The problem is that every other girl on the beach has noticed too. It's up to you to defend him, armed with just one thing: sandballs.

Beatch Slash! is a wave-based tower defense in pixel art, seen from above and set on a beach. The boyfriend doesn't move: he is the tower you have to protect.

## How to play

Move the heroine around the sand and throw sandballs at the girls heading your way, wave after wave. Every wave brings more opponents, and tougher than the last.

They won't hurt anyone: they flirt. Poses, glances and over-the-top, tongue-in-cheek advances, always in beachwear. But your boyfriend has two lines of defence:

- **Morality**: his moral backbone and his respect for you. The girls wear this down first.
- **Fidelity**: it comes into play once morality hits zero, and drops with every advance that lands.

If both reach zero, he gives in and runs off with the other girls. Game over.

## Sandro's Kiosk

Between waves you have about twenty seconds to dash to Sandro's Kiosk, the little beach bar. With the coins you've earned you can buy upgrades for yourself, restore your boyfriend's morality and fidelity, and try the day's boosts: a Beach Umbrella that halves the damage to morality, a Crossword Magazine to keep him busy, a Melting Ice Cream to double your coins. There's even a crab-shaped piggy bank.

## Where we are

The game is still in development. Right now it has menus, a tutorial, waves, a shop and game over; the level is a beach, with more planned. We are still working on:

- new types of opponents, from slow and sturdy to fragile and fast;
- balancing waves and prices;
- beach scenery, interface and animations.
