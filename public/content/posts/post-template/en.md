In this post we will talk about how this template page works. Every new article starts from here: copy the folder, change the title, date, text and images, and the site does the rest. The text is written in Markdown, so any plain text editor is enough.

![An image on the right, with the text flowing around it](immagine-1.svg "right")

## A section title

This paragraph flows around the image above. You can write in **bold**, in *italic* and add [a link](https://github.com). Images are positioned with a quoted title: `"right"` for the right, `"left"` for the left, or nothing for full width.
